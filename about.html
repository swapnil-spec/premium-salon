ABOUT OUR SALON

A salon experience with a human touch.

LUMÉA is a modern unisex salon created for people who want quality without the fuss. We work with men, women, boys and girls, offering everything from everyday grooming to occasion-ready beauty services.

Our approach is simple: listen carefully, recommend honestly and deliver beautifully. Every service begins with understanding what suits you — not simply following a trend.

Whether you're visiting for a quick trim, a complete hair transformation or a relaxing beauty treatment, our goal is for you to leave feeling refreshed, confident and cared for.
